DATUM Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

DATUM, including the iOS app and the web app, collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.

Settings are stored only on the device.

If you install a TestFlight build, crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Questions about this policy can be sent to netharman@icloud.com.
