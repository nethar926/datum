# DATUM Field Manual: Parts 3 and 4 chapter outline (acoustics)
Every plate comes from a seeded run or the sim's own model code; all platforms fictional.

## Part 3: Submarines and Surface Vessels
3.1 Propulsion plants: diesel-electric, AIP, nuclear; what each can and cannot radiate.
3.2 Operating states: surfaced, snorkelling, battery, AIP; which sources switch on/off in each.
3.3 Engine lines: crank rate, cylinder firing rate, engine firing rate and their harmonics.
3.4 Auxiliaries: engine-driven (fixed ratio to firing rate, bend together with RPM) vs independent electrical/AC/DC and intermittent (HYDP, compressors: fade, abrupt, knee on/off).
3.5 Shaft and propeller: PSR, blade rate, resonances sharing PSR spacing; reading them on DEMON.
3.6 Cavitation: onset vs speed and depth; broadband rise and DEMON modulation.
3.7 Speed-related components and flow noise.
3.8 Transients: periscope, planes/rudder, doors, dropped objects, class-specific.
3.9 Dynamic events: clutch/declutch, speed, course and depth changes, surface/dive; how each looks.
3.10 Class identity: shared engines across classes, one class across fictional navies.
3.11 Surface vessels: merchant, fishing, other; louder, rarely dynamic, how to tell them from subs.

## Part 4: The Ocean
4.1 Sound speed: temperature, salinity, pressure; the bathythermograph buoy and SSP plot.
4.2 Layers and thermocline: surface duct, shadow zones.
4.3 Propagation paths: direct, surface duct, bottom bounce, convergence zone.
4.4 Transmission loss: spreading and absorption; why high frequencies die first.
4.5 Lloyd's mirror: surface-image interference, fringe spacing vs depth/range/frequency; turning point at CPA on the ideal pass.
4.6 Ambient noise: sea state, shipping density, why the low band gets crowded.
4.7 Putting it together: why a contact fades in, band-dependent onset timing, and limited-data cases.
